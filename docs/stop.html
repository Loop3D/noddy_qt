Stop sign

In order to temporarily halt the structural history calculation short of the complete sequence, the Stop Sign Icon

may be placed anywhere after the initial Base Stratigraphy . Any structural or geophysical calculations will stop at the first Stop Sign found in the sequence.

Make sure to remove these Icons when the full history is required again!
